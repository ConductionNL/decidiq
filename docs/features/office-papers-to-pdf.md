# Office papers as PDF

A clerk adds a Word, Excel or PowerPoint file to a meeting or an agenda item.
decidiq turns it into a PDF within a few minutes. Members read the PDF, so
nobody needs the right Office app and everyone sees the same layout. The
original stays next to it for the secretariat.

## What happens when you add a paper

1. Add the file to the **Documents** of an agenda item or a meeting.
2. decidiq asks filinq to convert it in the background. The upload does not
   wait for it.
3. The PDF appears beside the original. The **Papers as PDF** widget shows the
   paper once, as its PDF.

The meeting package bundles the PDF, not the Office file.

## When a conversion fails

The original stays as it was. The widget shows the reason, for example
"Not converted: no backend could convert this file". The chair and the
secretary see **Try again** next to it.

## Switch it off

An administrator opens the decidiq admin settings and turns off **Convert Word,
Excel and PowerPoint papers to PDF when they are added**. New papers then stay
as they are added.

Conversion needs filinq. Without it, the admin settings say so and papers stay
as they are added.
