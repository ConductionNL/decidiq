# Nextcloud apps on your records

decidiq shows the Nextcloud apps you already use on the pages where they belong.
Each one appears only when that app is installed; without it, the page shows
everything else as before.

## Where each app appears

| Page | App | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| A meeting's integrations | Calendar | **Meeting calendar**: the meeting in your calendar, beside its action items, discussion, files and notes. |
| A participant | Contacts | **Contacts**: the person's address-book entries. |
| A governance body | Contacts | **Contacts**: the secretariat's and the members' address-book entries. |
| A decision's integrations | Polls | **Straw poll**: a quick poll on a draft decision. |
| A public consultation | Polls | **Straw poll**: a quick poll among the participants. |
| A public consultation | Forms | **Intake form**: the form that is the consultation's structured intake channel. |

Action-item deadlines get no calendar of their own: action items are tasks in
your Nextcloud calendar already, and the Tasks app and the action items board
show them.

## A straw poll is advisory

A straw poll is a sounding, not a vote. It never opens a voting round, never
records a vote and never changes a decision. When the result should count,
the chair opens a formal voting round and members vote there. The title
"Straw poll" says this on the page itself.

## Link a mail to a record

In Nextcloud Mail, open a message and use the OpenRegister panel beside it.
You can link the mail to a **meeting**, a **decision**, an **agenda item** or an
**action item**. The linked mail then shows on that record's Emails tab.

## Create a decision from a mail

From the same panel you can create a **decision** from a mail. It starts as a
draft resolution: the subject becomes its title, the start of the message its
text, and the mail is kept as its external reference. A draft is not published,
not put to a vote and sends no notifications until somebody moves it on.

Only decisions can be created from a mail. Action items cannot: they live in
your Nextcloud calendar, and decidiq does not write them.
