# Confidentiality

A body can keep an agenda item, a decision or a paper confidential for a
while. In Dutch local government this is *geheimhouding*: the council, the
chair or the executive board imposes it on a legal ground, the council
sometimes has to ratify it, and a later decision lifts it. This page shows how
to impose, ratify and lift a confidentiality restriction, and how to see who
opened the papers it covers.

## Impose confidentiality

1. Open the agenda item or the decision.
2. In the page's **Actions** menu, choose **Impose confidentiality**.
3. Pick the **Ground**. Each ground shows its name and, in brackets, the
   article it cites. Grounds an administrator switched off are not offered.
4. Pick who imposes it under **Imposed by**: the body, the chair or the
   executive board, and the **Governance body** it belongs to. On a decision
   the deciding body is filled in.
5. Choose **Impose confidentiality**.

The item or decision closes at once: it leaves the meeting pack and the
public site. Only the imposing body's chair, vice-chair or secretary, the
secretariat or an administrator may impose. Anyone else gets the reason why
in the dialog, and nothing is saved.

When the ground needs ratification, decidiq places a closed item on the
ratifying body's next open meeting and sets the ratification deadline to that
meeting's date.

## Ratify it

1. Open the restriction from **Confidentiality register**.
2. While it is imposed, choose **Ratify confidentiality** in the Actions menu.
3. Pick the **Ratifying decision** and confirm.

A restriction whose ground needs no ratification cannot be ratified; the
dialog says so.

## Lift it

1. Open the restriction.
2. While it is imposed or ratified, choose **Lift confidentiality**.
3. Pick the **Lifting decision**, and write the **Lifting conditions** if the
   decision sets any.
4. Confirm.

A restriction whose ground needs ratification cannot be lifted before it is
ratified. Once lifted, the item or decision can go through the normal
publication.

## See who opened the papers

The restriction page lists, under **Who opened the papers**, every opening of
a paper the restriction covers while it is active: who, when, which paper and
which agenda item, newest first. Only the imposing body's staff can read this
list. An opening after the restriction is lifted is not listed.

A paper opened straight from the Documents widget is not recorded yet; the
Papers widget on the agenda item is.
